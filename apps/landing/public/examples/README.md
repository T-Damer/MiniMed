# Landing image previews

192 × 192 grayscale renderings of slices 40, 52, 64, 76 and 88 (zero-based) from the repository’s
anonymized 3D Slicer samples in `apps/app/src/assets/example-mri.nii` and `example-ct.dcm`.
MRI display window: 0–160; CT display window: −1350–150. Nearest-neighbour sampling.
These are small demonstration images, not complete studies or diagnostic-quality exports.
No patient metadata is included. Source acknowledgements and licenses are copied alongside them.
