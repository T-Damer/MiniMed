# What the app still lacks: gap audit (GAP1, 2026-10-05)

Owner question (2026-10-05): «What do we lack in the current app? Some data missing maybe?» Read-only audit:
no code, data, pack or catalog was changed. Numbers are measured from the repository and the shipped packs
unless marked.

Legend: **M** measured today from repo data / packs; **L** live read-only request today (no login, no CAPTCHA,
one JSON endpoint of `apicr.minzdrav.gov.ru`, 5 sequential POSTs; one title search on `publication.pravo.gov.ru`);
**D** taken from a repo document, not re-measured; **E** my estimate. No Russian TLS root was installed; no
NSI/ЕСКЛП host was contacted (those need the disposable container of `docs/NSI_FETCH.md`).

## 0. Short answer

1. **The biggest daily-use gap is not missing КР text, it is finding it from a complaint or a case.** On the draft
   owner query set (54 queries, lexical «Все источники» path) hit@5 is 0.57 overall: 0.77 for rushed fragments but
   **0.30 for full clinical cases**. All 23 misses have the expected document in the shipped core (M). Complaint to КР
   is 0.04 lexical, 0.28 with the optional e5 model (D, Q1).
2. **Official КР are covered (763 of 764 current)**, drug identity is covered, and instruction text is reachable for
   97 % of drug positions (own 44 %, labelled same-substance fallback to 97 %).
3. **What is thin or absent is the "operational" layer a practising doctor uses next to the КР:** regulatory acts beyond
   paediatrics (33 two-kilobyte summary cards), the vaccination calendar as a usable table, lab reference ranges (one
   3.9 KB pilot), adult scores (50 calculators and 19 scales, none of NIHSS, CURB-65, NEWS2, Wells, SCORE2, GAD-7), structured
   safety views of drugs (pregnancy, renal, interactions), patient handouts, and most official forms (5 built).
4. **The data for several of these is already installed and unused:** 714 of 744 КР carry an official
   «Информация для пациента» appendix (median 2.5 KB, 3.3 MB in total).

## 2. Content gaps a practising Russian doctor hits

Each item: what is missing, evidence, lawful candidate source.

### 2.1 Care-profile orders, standards, prescribing rules
- **Evidence (M):** the regulatory pack has 33 cards; care-profile orders exist only for paediatrics (120н), neonatology
  (222н), paediatric endocrinology (583н), PMSP adults (202н) and children (313н), first aid (220н), infection in children
  (521н). Absent: therapy, cardiology, neurology, surgery, trauma, obstetrics, emergency (СМП), psychiatry, rehabilitation,
  palliative, anaesthesiology, prescription rules (1094н), narcotic/psychotropic lists, blood transfusion, sanitary rules.
  The need is already listed in `CONTENT_BACKLOG.md` «Нормативные документы на сбор». Стандарты медицинской помощи
  appear nowhere in the app (D, S1: 2017 open data is dead; new standards keep coming in 2026).
- **Source:** `publication.pravo.gov.ru` (official acts, robots allow, JSON API, used for forms F1). Scans without a text
  layer, so the F1 OCR route applies. Rights: official acts are not objects of copyright (ст. 1259 ГК, D).
- **Note:** cards are summaries by design (2 KB); decide whether full act text is wanted. Effort M per batch.

### 2.2 Vaccination calendar
- **Evidence (M):** card for order 1122н (6 chunks, 1 745 characters) says it «does not reproduce all age intervals»;
  appendix tables are not in the app. Latest federal acts on the portal (L, title search): 1122н of 06.12.2021 and its
  amendment 677н of 12.12.2023 (the card cites 677н); no newer federal order found by title. Raw data folder
  `data/raw/vaccination-handouts` holds US CDC VIS translations, not Russian-regulated schedules.
- **Source:** appendices 1 and 2 of 1122н (official, lawful). Output as a structured by-age calendar tool, not prose.

### 2.3 Lab reference ranges and interpretation
- **Evidence (M):** 3 864 characters in total. The disease-specific decision thresholds live inside КР text but there is no
  «what does this value mean» surface. Sources already in the private library (`Трактовка анализов.pdf`, Kapitan part 4,
  Шабалов neonatal) are `issues-open` in the review queue: no clinician spot-check, rights unchecked.
- **Candidate sources:** КР thresholds (official, lawful); CALIPER (published paediatric ranges, extended already);
  national adult ranges do not exist as one official set, so the lab's own interval must stay primary (existing rule).
- **Needs:** owner source decision and a clinician reviewer. Highest risk of silent error in this list.

### 2.4 Drug safety views: pregnancy, lactation, renal/hepatic, children, interactions
- **Evidence (M):** no structured data. Allmed has «Взаимодействие» in 2 732 of 4 708 cards and «Способ применения у детей»
  in 1 244; ГРЛС instructions hold the same as running text. `AGENTS.md` forbids inventing doses; the roadmap forbids
  hand-written symptom to drug dictionaries. The owner query «давление у беременной» fails today (M).
- **Candidate sources:** the section text of the official instructions already shipped (deterministic section split, shown per
  drug and as a cross-drug list, labelled by registration). No free structured pair-interaction source of acceptable rights
  was found in this repository (RLS, Видаль, ЛС ГЭОТАР forbid copying, D). NLM LactMed (English, US government) is a
  candidate for lactation only, and would need the machine-translation ADR (D5, not decided).

### 2.5 Emergency algorithms
- **Evidence (M):** КР exist for anaphylactic shock, sepsis, burns, poisonings, ventricular arrhythmia / sudden death. No КР
  title matches resuscitation (СЛР). The first-aid order 220н is a summary card.
- **Candidate sources:** КР appendices «Алгоритмы действий врача» (installed, per КР); СЛР from Минздрав acts (lawful) or the
  Russian Resuscitation Council (rights unchecked). Effort S for КР algorithm views, M for СЛР.

### 2.6 МКБ-10 completeness and provenance
- **Evidence (M, D):** 9 835 RLS pages against 15 038 official НСИ rows; RLS terms forbid redistribution (owner accepted
  for personal use, 2026-09-28). The official dictionary has a free-text index of 81 438 rows that would feed search.
- **Source:** НСИ МКБ-10 `1.2.643.5.1.13.13.11.1005` and index `…1489`, via the disposable-container method used for ATC (N1).

### 2.7 Adult calculators and scales
- **Evidence (M):** grep over `content/tool-modules`, calculators and assessments finds no NIHSS, mRS, CURB-65, NEWS2,
  qSOFA/SOFA, Wells, Padua, Glasgow-Blatchford, HEART, SCORE2, MELD, GAD-7, PHQ-9, MMSE, MoCA, Barthel, Braden. All appear in
  `CONTENT_BACKLOG.md` as candidates; several have an official КР source slice already extracted (stroke 814_1, ACS 154_4,
  AF 382_2, HF 156_2, depression 301, schizophrenia 451).
- **Source:** КР appendices (official, lawful) and original publications. Licences: MMSE and MoCA are not free to
  reproduce (E, verify); PHQ-9 and GAD-7 are free to use (E, verify). Needs a Russian-version validity note per scale.

### 2.8 Forms beyond 274н
- **Evidence (D):** `FORMS_PLAN.md` verified 057/у (519н, in force, 1 to 1.5 days), 058/у (740н, in force only from
  2027-03-01), 088/у, 003-В/у, 071/у, 002-О/у, 003-О/у, Rx blanks (1094н). 086/у, 027/у, 095/у, 063/у have no official
  source on the portal. Since 2026-09-01 order 286н allows free-form certificates, so some documents are drafts only.
  Layout-fidelity overlay test (owner requirement) is not done.

### 2.9 Patient handouts
- **Evidence (M):** 714 of 744 installed КР contain a «Информация для пациента» appendix (median 2.5 KB, 3.3 MB overall).
  `docs/TODO.md` «Памятки для пациентов» is a proposal awaiting approval. The app can print reference documents already.
- **Source:** the КР appendices themselves (official), plus doctor uploads. Effort S to M, no new data.

### 2.10 КР freshness and editions
- **Evidence (L, M):** two current editions appeared in three days (172_4 on 10-02, 1081_1 on 10-05). The next release
  needs a quick check per source (R1), an edition diff view and re-anchored highlights; none exist. Pointer pages still
  do not read `supersededByDocumentId` (D, CORE2 «Not verified»).

### 2.11 Items checked and already present
Vaccination order card (summary only), diets (6 of 15), ATC names (N1), country of manufacture (MFG1), colloquial
aliases, 5 forms plus update checker, sick leave orders, WHO growth z-scores, CKD-EPI and Schwartz, patient diary.

## 5. Caveats

- The owner query set is a draft written by an agent for the owner to review, KR-heavy (53 of about 100 targets), and the
  report in `data/build/owner-queries-report.json` was run at 21:22 on 2026-10-05 while the working tree held uncommitted
  S3 name-variant and МКБ-bridge changes; treat the baseline as approximate.
- Rights are not legal advice; rows marked E need verification before any shipment.
- Not verified: any clinical content, the real device, the live rights terms of the RLS and krasotaimedicina sources.
