- `matricaria-chamomilla` — pregnancy split into preparation a) (tea: established) and b)–o) (extracts:
  not recommended); infant line reworded to «не обоснован»; sedative/coumarin constituent lines cut to
  the quote.
- `hypericum-perforatum` — depression indication names preparations a), b); GI indication separated;
  age split (oral under 18, cutaneous under 12, both quoted); contraindication quote covers the full

- `senna-alexandrina` — dose, warning, adverse (split in three) and overdose re-quoted; «настой»
  removed from the preparation (the monograph says «herbal preparation»).

other 25 files were not re-read and likely carry the same kind of additions; they need the same check
before a clinician review.

## EMA re-check of the other 25 files (HERB1-QA2)

Every fact and constituent of the 25 files the spot check skipped was read against its cited PDF page
(pymupdf text of the page and its neighbours; five Sonnet readers, five files each, diffs reviewed and
sampled against the pages by claude-opus). All 30 files pass `check-herbal-ema`; `status` stays
`proposed`. About 270 items changed. The same additions as in the spot check turned up in every file.

Changes common to most files:
- «данных нет» → «данных недостаточно» («in the absence of sufficient data») in the pregnancy line.
- `populationRu` now follows the posology heading of its column: «взрослые и пожилые», «подростки,
  взрослые и пожилые», «подростки старше 12 лет…», «взрослые и пожилые женщины» (uva-ursi). Two
  quotes were extended back to the heading.
- Age lines that read as permission («не младше 12 лет») now carry the source modal («детям до
  12 лет не рекомендуется»).
- Rules that the monograph gives for one indication only are now labelled with it: duration, fluid
  intake, fever/dysuria warning and adverse lines (equisetum, tilia, achillea, potentilla, quercus).
- Reasons, advice and glosses taken from neighbouring sentences: the quote was extended to cover them
  or the Russian was trimmed. Constituent effects stated as fact were brought back to what the
  sentence says: «приписывают», «по выводу авторов», the species and test, in vitro or animal.

Per file:
- `achillea-millefolium` (11): «примочки», «повязки» and the wrong GI indication group removed or
  re-quoted. Constituent luteolin-7-O-glucoside corrected to the glucuronide. The achillicin and
  oedema constituents re-quoted.
- `arctostaphylos-uva-ursi` (9): indication restored as «relief of symptoms». «Холодный настой» →
  «настой или мацерат». The men warning no longer says «без наблюдения врача». The arbutin
  metabolite constituent evidence changed from clinical to in-vitro. The tannin line re-quoted.
- `artemisia-absinthium` (4): population fixed. The bitters constituent is recast on its saliva study
  (absinthin was not in the span). The thujone GABA and 6 mg limit lines re-quoted.
- `betula-pendula` (6): fluid intake split into its own posology fact. «Обычно курс» → «традиционно
  2–4 недели». The flavonoid diuretic line now says the results are inconclusive.
- `calendula-officinalis` (9): the age modals and the Asteraceae contraindication fixed. The
  wound-healing constituent claimed «ускоряют заживление»; the source says keratinocyte migration
  was «only marginally influenced» and collagenase inhibition «only moderate», and the line now says so.
- `centaurium-erythraea` (4): «возбуждают аппетит» removed from the secoiridoids. The antibacterial
  line names the bacteria.
- `equisetum-arvense` (11): duration, contraindication, warning, pregnancy and adverse lines tied to
  indication 1. The adverse line gained the GI complaints through a quote extension. The silicic
  acid, decoction (rabbits) and diuretic constituents re-quoted.
- `eucalyptus` (9): inhalation quote extended. «Препараты с цинеолом» in the under-30-months line.
  The cineole, macrocarpal and tannin effects hedged as in the source.
- `foeniculum-vulgare` (9): duration «взрослым и подросткам». The estragole limit carries the
  1.0 µg/kg guidance value. Anethole in milk changed to «есть данные». «Причина ограничений» removed
  from the estragole constituent. The anethole gastric-emptying line now says «у мышей».
- `frangula-alnus` (15): the adverse line split in three (pseudomelanosis, electrolyte imbalance,
  urine colour). Dose, warning and overdose re-quoted as in senna. «Отвар или настой» → «препарат».
  «Кратковременно» dropped from the indication (see the limits below).
- `glycyrrhiza-glabra` (17): «токсичность для потомства» re-quoted to the reproductive-toxicity
  sentence. The lactation line now has its «not recommended» sentence. The CYP3A4 midazolam and
  omeprazole finding quoted. The cardiovascular warning and the other-liquorice-products line
  re-quoted. The glycyrrhetinic acid «задержка воды, потеря калия» claim removed.
- `juniperus-communis` (9): the fluid-intake reason quoted. The diuretic constituent's evidence
  changed from review to animal (rats). The nephrotoxicity line follows its sentence.
- `leonurus-cardiaca` (9): the lactation quote ended mid-sentence. «Спиртовые препараты» removed
  from the driving warning: the source names no preparation. The constituent list and the sedative
  study (not confirmed by modern methods) re-quoted.
- `linum-usitatissimum` (12): «трудности с глотанием» split into its own contraindication. The
  oestrogen warning, interaction timing, pregnancy and overdose lines re-quoted to their full
  sentences. The mucilage preparation quote now carries «за полчаса до еды».
- `melissa-officinalis` (9): tea population «подростки старше 12 лет…». The sedative constituent
  says «у мышей, в/б». The GABA constituent says in vitro. The duration sentence is split by a
  footnote across pages 4/5 and stays as two consecutive lines («…дольше 2 недель…» / «…во время
  применения — к врачу или медработнику»).
- `mentha-piperita` (10): the reflux caution quoted. Constituent effects stated as the source does
  (antiemetic «по выводу авторов», guinea-pig ileum, choleretic in dogs, carminative in vitro).
- `pimpinella-anisum` (9): «семян» → «плодов». Tea population with the heading. The
  Apiaceae/anethole contraindication added. The expectorant gloss replaced by the source terms. The
  cat study named.
- `potentilla-erecta` (11): oral doses tied to diarrhoea. «Настоем или отваром» for rinses.
  Interaction «за 1 час и более». The tannin lines say the effect is presumed, with no experimental
  data.
- `quercus-robur` (9): «отвар» removed from the oral dose (comminuted bark in water). The bath split
  into its dose and «одна полная или частичная ванна в день»: the old «не чаще раза» was stronger
  than the source. Duration tied to its indications.
- `salvia-officinalis` (12): «длительное применение возможно» quoted. «В больших дозах токсичен»,
  «причина запрета» and the GABA gloss removed. The tea frequency was dropped from the dose (see
  the limits below).
- `taraxacum-officinale` (16): the bile reason and the fluid-intake reason quoted, page corrected.
  «За полчаса до еды» quoted. The taraxinic acid glucoside split into an aspirin-lesion (rat) entry
  and a contact-sensitizer entry.
- `tilia-cordata` (10): age modals fixed. Duration and warning tied to indication 1. The anxiolytic
  fraction is «вероятно флавоноидной природы» (Tilia tomentosa, mice).
- `urtica-dioica` (9): «caffeic malic acid» as in the quote. The NF-κB and gonarthritis lines name
  IDS23 and the rat model.
- `valeriana-officinalis` (13): «не для быстрого эффекта» quoted («gradual onset»). Duration lines
  marked as well-established use. 6-methylapigenin evidence changed from in-vitro to animal
  (plus-maze). «Корня», «снижать внимание» and other glosses trimmed.
- `zea-mays` (8): «между едой» split into its own posology fact. The diuretic mechanism now reads
  «по выводу авторов».

After the readers finished, the stiffest calques were smoothed: «квалифицированному специалисту
здравоохранения» → «медработнику», the driving sentence, and «гиперчувствительность к действующему

## Page breaks and missing monograph lines (HERB1-QA3)

The open points above, closed except for the salvia thujone line and the clinician review.

Quote check (`herbal_ema.py`): a quote that starts on its page and runs on to the next one is now also
matched with the page cut before each line that opens a footnote (`3 For guidance on herbal substance…`),
and the next page's header block — the leading blank or repeated lines up to «Page N/M» — is dropped even
when the running title wraps differently on some pages (the fennel assessment report wraps it three ways,
so no single variant reached the half-of-pages threshold). A quote that skips real body text is still
refused (`test_quote_runs_on_past_a_footnote_and_a_running_title`).

Quotes made whole (each had been cut at a page break):
- `frangula-alnus`, `senna-alexandrina` indication: «Herbal medicinal product for short-term use in cases of
  occasional constipation.», page 3; «Кратковременно» is back in frangula and now has a quote in senna.
- `foeniculum-vulgare` estragole constituent: «…liver carcinogens in both these tests.»; Russian «в обоих
  опытах на животных».
- `melissa-officinalis` duration: the two lines are one fact again, quoted whole.
- `salvia-officinalis` tea for indication 1: «…as herbal infusion 3 times daily.»; `timesPerDay: 3` back.

Monograph lines that had no fact, now quoted exactly (`status: proposed`):

- `linum-usitatissimum`: traditional-use duration (1 week, then a doctor); debilitated and elderly patients
